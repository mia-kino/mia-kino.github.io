RM Discovery (personal)

RM Discovery is a personal, non-commercial 4K release monitor. It is operated by Mia for her own use only. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

What it does: on a schedule, it checks publicly available product listings for new and upcoming 4K Ultra HD disc releases and keeps a record of what it found.

Google account access: the app asks Google for one permission only, the Google Drive scope "drive.file". That scope lets the app see, create and change only the Google Drive files that the app itself creates. The app uses it to save its own records in a folder it creates in the operator's own Google Drive. It cannot see or open any other file in that Drive.

The app shares no data with anyone, sells nothing, shows no advertising and uses no analytics or trackers.
