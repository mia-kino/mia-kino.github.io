Privacy Policy

App: RM Discovery (personal). Effective October 9, 2026.

Who runs the app

RM Discovery is a personal, non-commercial 4K release monitor operated by Mia for her own use. It has one user, the operator. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google user data the app accesses

The app requests one Google permission only: the Google Drive scope "drive.file" (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file). With it, the app can see, create and change only the Google Drive files that the app itself creates. It cannot see, open or change any other file in the operator's Google Drive. The app does not request access to email, contacts, calendar, photos or any other Google data.

How the data is used and stored

The app creates one folder in the operator's own Google Drive and saves its own records there: records of publicly available product listings it has checked, and its own working state. It reads back only those files, to check that they were saved correctly and to continue its work on the next run. The authorization token that lets the app reach that folder is kept as an encrypted secret in the operator's private automation settings and is never published.

Sharing

The app shares no data with anyone. It does not sell data, show advertising, use analytics or trackers, or transfer Google user data to any third party. This website sets no cookies and runs no scripts.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The app's files stay in the operator's own Google Drive until the operator deletes them. The operator can delete them at any time in Google Drive.

How to revoke access

Sign in to your Google Account and open the list of third-party apps that have access to it (in current menus "Access to your Google Account" at myaccount.google.com/linkedapps; older menus call it "Third-party apps and services"). Select RM Discovery (personal), then remove access and confirm. The app then can no longer reach Google Drive. Files it already created stay in the Drive until deleted there.

Changes

If what the app does with Google data changes, this page will be updated first.
